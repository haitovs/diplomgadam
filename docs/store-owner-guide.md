# Guide for restaurant owners

How to get your restaurant listed and keep it up to date.

## Registering

Go to **For restaurants** and choose **Register**. You will need:

- Your name and role at the restaurant
- A phone number — **this is how you sign in**, so use one you keep
- A password of at least 10 characters
- The restaurant's name and public phone number
- A primary language: the one you will definitely fill in

Your listing is created as a draft. Nobody can see it yet.

### About the primary language

The site is in Turkmen, English and Russian. You must complete your primary
language; the other two are optional.

If you leave a translation out, visitors reading in that language see your
primary-language text instead. Nothing is ever blank. Fill in the others when
you have time — the language tabs on each field show which are still missing.

## Completing the listing

The dashboard lists exactly what is still needed. Each item links to the right
page. You need:

| Required | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Restaurant name | Details |
| Description | Details |
| Address | Details |
| Location on the map | Details — click the map to place the pin |
| Phone number | Details |
| At least one cuisine | Details |
| Cover photo | Photos |
| Photos of the venue | Photos |
| Opening hours | Opening hours |

Worth adding as well: delivery and takeaway, amenities such as parking, wifi, a
family section or a banquet hall, payment methods, seating capacity, and your
Instagram or Telegram. Visitors filter by these, so a listing that fills them in
gets found more often.

### Opening hours

Set each day separately, or set one day and use the copy button for the rest.

**A day can have two periods.** If you close in the afternoon and reopen in the
evening, add a second time range rather than pretending you are open all day.

**Closing after midnight works.** For a restaurant open 18:00 until 02:00, enter
exactly that — a closing time earlier than the opening time is understood as
running past midnight.

**Special days** handle holidays and one-off changes without disturbing your
normal week.

### Photos

Three kinds, and the difference matters:

- **Cover photo** — the main image visitors see in search results. Choose your
  best one.
- **Gallery** — interior, dishes, atmosphere. Up to 12.
- **Verification photos** — pictures of the building and inside. **These are
  never shown publicly.** They exist so an administrator can confirm the
  restaurant is real and yours. Without them your listing cannot be submitted.

Photos are resized and optimised automatically, so upload straight from your
phone. Up to 8 MB per photo.

### The menu

Create sections in the order you want them shown — Breakfast, Grills, Drinks —
then add dishes inside each. Use the arrows to reorder sections.

Each dish has a name, an optional description, a price and an optional photo.
Type prices as manat: `45` or `45.50` (`45,50` also works).

Dishes you have run out of can be marked unavailable rather than deleted; they
disappear from the public menu and come back with one click.

## Submitting for review

When nothing is left in the checklist, press **Submit for review**.

An administrator checks your listing. **They will usually telephone the number
you gave** to confirm the restaurant is real and that you are connected to it,
so make sure it is answered.

If something is wrong, you get a specific reason on your dashboard. Fix it and
submit again.

Once approved, your restaurant is on the site.

## After you are published

**Your changes go live immediately.** No further review. Prices change, dishes
come and go, and none of that should wait for anyone.

Keep these current:

- Prices — an out-of-date price is worse than no price
- Availability of dishes
- Opening hours, especially around holidays
- Your phone number

Closing for a few weeks? Use **Temporarily closed** with a short note instead of
deleting anything. Your listing stays visible and marked as closed, and one click
brings it back.

## Signing in and passwords

Sign in with your phone number and password. Any format works: `65123456`,
`+993 65 123456`, `993 65 123456`.

Change your password under **Account**.

**If you forget it,** telephone the platform administrator. They can issue a
temporary password, which you change on your next sign-in. There is no automatic
reset by email.

After too many wrong attempts, sign-in is blocked for 15 minutes. Wait, or call
the administrator.

## If your listing is suspended

You can still sign in and read the reason, but not edit. Contact the
administrator, resolve the issue, and they will reinstate it.
